# Improving click-through rate (CTR)

## Diagnose first
A low CTR with high impressions usually means the ad is shown to the wrong audience or the creative is weak. Compare CTR against the account median before changing anything.

## Creative levers
Test one variable at a time: headline, call to action, or image. Run each variant until it has at least 1,000 impressions before judging. Headlines that mention a concrete benefit outperform generic brand statements.

## Keyword relevance
Remove broad keywords that attract irrelevant clicks. Add long-tail keywords that match purchase intent. Keyword-to-ad-copy alignment is the cheapest CTR improvement.
