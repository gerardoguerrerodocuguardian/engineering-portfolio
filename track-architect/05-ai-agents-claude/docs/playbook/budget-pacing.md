# Budget pacing and exhausted campaigns

## Symptoms of poor pacing
A campaign marked EXHAUSTED before midday is spending its daily budget too fast. Impressions are concentrated in the early hours and late-day traffic is lost.

## Fixes
Lower the max bid so the budget lasts longer, or raise the daily budget if the return on spend is positive. Prefer even pacing over accelerated delivery unless the offer is time-limited.

## Guardrail
Never raise a daily budget by more than 20 percent in one step; review results for 48 hours before the next change.
