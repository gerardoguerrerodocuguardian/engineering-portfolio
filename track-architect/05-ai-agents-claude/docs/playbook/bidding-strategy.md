# Bidding strategy basics

## Max bid
The max bid is the highest price per click the advertiser will pay. Setting it too low reduces impressions; setting it too high wastes spend on low-intent clicks.

## Cost per click
Track spend divided by clicks. If cost per click rises while CTR falls, the campaign is competing in an auction it should not be in. Narrow the keywords instead of bidding higher.

## Paused campaigns
A PAUSED campaign accrues no spend. Before reactivating, review its keywords and bid against current benchmarks.
