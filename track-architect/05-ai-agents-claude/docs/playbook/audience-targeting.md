# Audience targeting

## Narrow before you widen
Start with a tight audience and expand only once conversions are stable. Overly broad targeting dilutes CTR and inflates cost per click.

## Exclusions
Exclude existing customers from acquisition campaigns. Review placement reports monthly and exclude low-quality placements.

## Frequency
Showing the same ad too often causes fatigue. Rotate creatives when frequency is high and CTR trends down.
